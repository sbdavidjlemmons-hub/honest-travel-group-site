While you wait

A few things we've learned about traveling well.

Free advice, no strings — whether or not you ever book anything with us.

🌅
For couples

Travel the shoulder weeks

  • Book the two or three weeks either side of peak season. Same weather, noticeably lower rates, and far fewer people in every photo you take.
  • If you both work remote, fly in a day early and treat day one as a workday. Cheaper flight, and you wake up on vacation instead of spending your first morning in transit.
  • Plan one thing per day. Two things is a schedule. One thing is a vacation.
🏡
For grandparents

Book the kitchen, not the room count

  • A condo with a kitchen and a living room is what makes three generations actually enjoy a week together. Separate hotel rooms turn into separate vacations.
  • Get everyone on one arrival day even if somebody has to take a connection. Split arrivals quietly eat a day and a half.
  • Leave one afternoon completely unplanned. That is reliably the afternoon everyone remembers.
🎒
For families

Give every kid one day to run

  • Let each child own the plan for one day. Ownership beats itinerary, and the complaining basically stops.
  • Pack a first-night bag — swimsuits, chargers, one change of clothes each. The pool happens before the unpacking, not after.
  • Take the family photo on day one. Everybody looks better before the sunburn.
