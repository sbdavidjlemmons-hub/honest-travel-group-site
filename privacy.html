Privacy Policy

How we handle your information — plainly.

Last updated: August 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Honest Vacation Marketing, a brand of Elite Marketing Solutions LLC ("we," "us"), collects, uses, and protects information you provide on honestvacationmarketing.com.

Information we collect

When you complete a form on our site we collect the information you provide: your name, mobile phone number, email address, and your answers to qualification questions. We also record the date, time, page URL, and consent language version at the moment you submit, along with standard technical data (such as UTM parameters) that tell us which ad or link brought you here.

How we use it

We use your information to contact you about the promotional vacation offer you requested, to confirm and remind you about your scheduled live presentation, to answer your questions, and to share related travel offers. We contact you only through the channels you consented to.

SMS / text messaging

By checking the consent box on our form, you agree to receive marketing and transactional text messages from Honest Vacation Marketing at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Mobile information and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Text-message originator opt-in data is not shared with any third parties.

How we share it

We share your information only with the fulfillment provider needed to deliver the offer you requested (Customer Service Network Group, a registered Seller of Travel) and with service providers who help us operate (such as our CRM). We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your SMS opt-in with third parties.

Your choices

You may opt out of texts by replying STOP, unsubscribe from email using the link in any message, or ask us to delete your information by emailing info@honestvacationmarketing.com.
