Worth knowing either way

Three things I'd tell a friend booking this exact trip.

Right for you if you want to know the number before you go and not think about money again until you're home. These are useful whether you ever book anything with me or not — that's rather the point.

Make the reservation restaurants your default.

“All-inclusive” buffets feed everyone; the à la carte rooms feed the people who booked at 9am. Reserve every specialty restaurant on day one, then cancel what you don't want.

Tip in small bills anyway.

It's included, technically. Five singles on day one at the swim-up bar buys you the best service of the week — not because it's required, because it's remembered.

Check the resort's day-pass price.

Whatever they charge outsiders for a day is roughly what your included day is worth. It reframes the whole week, and tells you which resort in the program is the real value.
