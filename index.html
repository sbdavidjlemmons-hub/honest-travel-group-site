Who we are

A qualifying company — for travelers and for the programs that serve them.

We understand what it’s like to be told you’re getting something and then not getting it when you get there. We know what it’s like to be in the membership rooms — selling programs to people who have no idea what they’re being offered. So we qualify everyone: travelers, programs, and presenters. We get the right details to the right people, so the right offers get presented. No hard feelings.

We only provide amazing offers from amazing companies, and we only offer amazing vacations for amazing travelers. Enjoy.

What we do

Three jobs. All done in-house.

✔️

Qualify travelers for membership presentations

Every qualifying question is asked before a seat is booked — couple, age, income, credit card — so presenters meet couples who already fit the program's own rules.

🎟️

Operate certificate offers end-to-end

Four certificate families, forty destinations. Every traveler-paid charge is printed before the form, and the certificate is issued the moment a session is booked.

🔒

Compliance and data rails owned in-house

Consent language, disclosures, attribution and lead records run on our own systems — one source of truth from the first click to the booked seat.

The other side of travel

Stuck in a timeshare? We do the exact same thing on the way out.

Getting out of a timeshare is where travelers get hurt most — by the same kind of room, the same kind of promise. So we qualify the exit side the way we qualify everything: we put the Group’s name on the line for honesty and transparency, and we hold accountable the ones who won’t. We control the flow because we put the consumer first. One rule, both directions.
