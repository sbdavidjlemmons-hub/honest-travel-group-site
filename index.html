From the founder

Twenty years in this industry, and I finally figured out the fix.

Marketing, telemarketing, audience strategy, sales leadership — I've done all of it, and I spent most of it watching genuinely good travel programs get sold with tactics that made people distrust the whole category. The programs weren't the problem. The way they were sold was.

So here's the fix, and it's almost embarrassingly simple: print everything first. Every tax, every fee, the refundable deposit, the market price we're beating, and the fact that yes — there's a presentation. All of it, on the page, before you give me your name.

I want parents watching their kids see the ocean for the first time. I want couples reconnecting and grandparents making memories with grandkids. That's what a vacation is actually for — and nobody should have to fight through fine print to get there.

— Dave Lemmons, Founder · The Honest Travel Group
