How people opt in to text messages from us.

This page exists so a carrier or messaging reviewer can see the entire opt-in flow in one place, without hunting through a marketing page. Everything below is rendered from the same source as the live forms.

2. Where the opt-in appears

Opt-in is collected by web form only. The form appears twice on each of these four pages — once near the top, once in full at the bottom. Both instances carry the identical consent language shown below.

  • https://honestvacationmarketing.com/cruise.html
  • https://honestvacationmarketing.com/all-inclusive.html

  • https://honestvacationmarketing.com/condo.html

3. The exact controls the consumer sees

Reproduced here from the same code that renders the live form. The first box is required to request a certificate. The SMS consent box is separate, optional, and unchecked by default — the certificate is issued either way, so consent is never a condition of purchase.

A mobile number is optional on the form. If the consent box is ticked, the phone field becomes required — consent is only ever recorded against an actual number, never against a blank field.

4. What the consumer sees after submitting

On submit, the visitor is taken to a confirmation page at https://honestvacationmarketing.com/thank-you.html showing their certificate number and their session booking step. The submission is recorded with a timestamp and the version of the consent language in force at that moment.

5. What we send

Transactional confirmations and reminders for the session the consumer booked, plus occasional promotional offers if they ticked the optional box. Message frequency varies and is low — typically a booking confirmation, a reminder, and infrequent offers thereafter.

Honest Vacation Marketing: You're in, David — live Vacation Savings Preview, Tue 1:30 PM PT. Zoom link is in your email. Reply YES to confirm your seat, or reply and we'll rebook you. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Honest Vacation Marketing: reminder — your Vacation Savings Preview starts in 30 minutes. Same Zoom link from your email. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

6. How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to opt out immediately. Reply HELP for help, or contact (855) 418-6714 / info@honestvacationmarketing.com. Message and data rates may apply. Full detail in our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Mobile information and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Text-message originator opt-in data is not shared with any third parties.

7. What we do not do

We hold a purchased marketing audience used strictly for advertising targeting and email. No SMS is ever sent to those records. An appended phone number is not consent. The only numbers that receive text messages are those where a person entered their own number on the form above and ticked the optional consent box.
