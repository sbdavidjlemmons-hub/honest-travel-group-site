About & mission

We're restoring honesty to how people book travel.

Founded by Dave Lemmons after 20+ years inside the industry.

The Honest Travel Group exists because the vacation industry earned its bad reputation one hidden fee at a time. Great membership programs and real travel value got buried under deceptive marketing, surprise costs, and high-pressure sales tables. People stopped trusting the offer before they ever heard it.

The diagnosis

Dave Lemmons has spent more than twenty years in marketing, telemarketing, audience strategy, and sales leadership — watching honest programs sold with dishonest tactics. The result: a market so full of pop-ups, fine print, and confusion that planning a single trip can eat three days of your life.

The fix

Everything upfront, everything optional. Every certificate offer prints its taxes, fees, deposit, and honest market value before we ask for your name. Yes, there's a 45–60 minute presentation — we say so in the first sentence, and you get the trip either way. A few questions, the best package we can build, and options across the board. AI-supported for speed, with a real human always one click away.

The mission

People are going to travel forever, and the industry only grows. So we fix the gap instead of exploiting it. We want you to travel with us, your family to travel with us, and your kids and grandkids to carry the memories forward. Vacations should be about enjoying your family's time — not stressing over what you got charged for.

And for the folks who got pushed into a timeshare they never wanted, Honest Timeshare Resolutions walks them out the same way — their pace, their options, their call.

— Dave Lemmons, Founder · The Honest Travel Group
