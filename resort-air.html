Worth knowing either way

Three things I'd tell a friend booking this exact trip.

Right for you if airfare is the line item that usually kills the trip before it starts. These are useful whether you ever book anything with me or not — that's rather the point.

Fly Tuesday or Wednesday, both directions.

Same route, same airline, routinely $80–140 cheaper per seat than Friday/Sunday. On two tickets that's most of a rental car.

Base fare only — plan for it.

The credit applies to base fare, so pick the fare class where base is the biggest slice: economy on a mainline carrier, not “basic” on a discounter where half the price is fees the credit can't touch.

Fly in the day before.

If you both work remote, day one from the resort beats a 5am departure. Cheaper flight, and you wake up already there.
