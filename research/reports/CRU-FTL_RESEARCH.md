## Risk notes
- **Single-retailer dependence.** All 14 priced rows come from one online agency. Every cruise-line-direct cross-check failed for technical reasons (rows 17–18) — not because the fares disagreed, but because the pages serve no price to a server-side fetch. A second independent retailer should be added before this figure is treated as settled.
- **No 2027 Royal Caribbean fare could be verified.** Six separate Royal Caribbean 2027 sailings from Port Everglades returned zero across all cabin categories (rows 15–16 and notes). This directly undercuts the certificate page's existing claim that "current 3-, 4-, and 5-night inventory verified primarily on Royal Caribbean" for the forward window.
- **The existing on-card "$700–$2,100" range is not supported as stated.** Its floor is above the verified low ($650.06) and its ceiling is reachable only through Disney Cruise Line inventory. If that range stays on the page, it should be re-sourced or narrowed to the mainstream band actually verified: roughly $650–$1,225.
- **The 45-day notice rule prunes the near-term set.** Rows 2, 5 and 7 depart inside 45 days of today and are market context only, not certificate-bookable. Excluding them does not weaken the recommendation — the mainstream upper quartile actually rises to $1,125.33 — but any citation of those rows must be labeled.
- **Only 5-night oceanview verification is Disney-only.** The certificate's charge baseline uses the 5-night top of range ($157 pp), yet the only verified 5-night oceanview pairings from Port Everglades in-window are Disney (rows 13–14). Mainstream 5-night oceanview inventory could not be priced. The $314 charge figure is therefore conservative against a set that is mostly 3- and 4-night.
- **Fare volatility.** Cruise fares are dynamic and inventory-driven; all rows are a single point in time (2026-09-09). None is a locked or guaranteed rate.
- **Product identity.** The certificate guarantees no line, ship, itinerary, port or date. Any comparison must be framed as a category comparison, never as "the same cruise." Display outputs A and B above are written to that standard.
- **Image brand exposure.** No real cruise-ship photograph can be fully brand-neutral — the ship is the operator's trade dress. The alternate (sunset silhouette) carries the lowest legibility risk and should be preferred if the page's "representative vessel" disclaimer is considered insufficient.
- **Image framing unconfirmed at pixel level.** Downloading was prohibited, so port framing rests on geocoordinates, titles, category placement and file descriptions. Confirm visually before publish.
