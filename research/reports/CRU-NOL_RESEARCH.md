## Risk notes
- **The 3-night promise cannot be delivered from New Orleans.** Carnival's live inventory for departure port MSY returns only 4-Day and 5-Day itineraries; Royal Caribbean and Norwegian sail 7 nights and longer from New Orleans; Disney has no New Orleans season in 2027. The certificate's "3, 4, or 5 nights" language is unsupported at the 3-night end for this port. It is also the length that carries the lowest traveler charge ($145 pp), so a buyer reading "3 nights" is reading a price point that does not exist here.
- **Single-ship dependency.** Every priced comparable is Carnival Valor. If that ship is redeployed, drydocked, or its New Orleans season is shortened, the entire comparable set and the certificate's fulfilment path move together. Carnival Dream replaces Valor on the New Orleans short product from May 16, 2027 at a materially higher lead fare (from $678 pp, row 14), which will push the comparable up but also raises the cost of fulfilment.
- **The existing on-card range overstates the top.** The certificate currently shows "$750–$1,800 for 2 adults in an outside cabin." The highest oceanview total verified in the certificate's own usable window is **$1,382** (row 9). Recommend narrowing the displayed range to roughly **$800–$1,400**, or replacing it with the single $1,346 figure.
- **Taxes, fees and port expenses are excluded from every priced row and their amount is unverified.** No fetched page disclosed them, and reseller "taxes included" claims are internally inconsistent (row 15). Any public-facing copy must keep "taxes and applicable fees apply" and must not imply the comparable is an all-in number.
- **Outside/oceanview is the scarce class on this ship.** It was SOLD OUT on three of the sailings checked while interior and balcony remained available. A certificate promising specifically an outside stateroom on 45 days' notice is exposed here.
- **Not like-for-like on inclusions.** Gratuities, transport to New Orleans, excursions, beverages and upgrades are additional on both sides, so the $1,346 comparable and the $314 certificate charge are both partial trip costs. The disclaimer already on the page covers this and should stay.
- **Holiday exclusion applied.** The Dec 26, 2026 sailings (oceanview $768 pp / 4N and $687 pp / 5N) were dropped as holiday sailings the certificate excludes. Including them would have raised the premium figure — leaving them out is the conservative choice.
- **Image caveat.** No image file was opened. Logo legibility on both nominations is inferred, not observed, and must be confirmed on screen before either goes to production. Both nominated files are ShareAlike; the reserve file (CC BY 2.0) is the lower-friction licence if ShareAlike becomes a problem.
