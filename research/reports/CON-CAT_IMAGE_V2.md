## The supply finding, stated plainly

**The Catskills carry no compliant condo/resort exterior in free stock. Not one.**

C2 predicted "one-or-none" for a small inland destination. For the Catskills the answer is **none**.
32 candidates were opened individually plus 9 more on a Flickr contact sheet. Every built-inventory
frame that was actually in the Catskills failed, and every frame that looked like condo inventory
turned out to be somewhere else:

- The one frame that photographed as a genuine multi-storey condominium beside a lake under an
  autumn ridge (Pexels 34653928) is **Mont-Tremblant, Québec**, per its own Pexels page. It is
  tagged "Condominium / Condo" — a textbook C6 destination solvent pulling Québec into a Catskills
  search.
- A five-frame series of a pretty settlement under peak foliage (Marta Wave, Pexels 5875861/73/94/
  5875924/31) that surfaced in *four* separate Catskills queries is **Wilmington, New York
  (Whiteface, Adirondacks)** — proven by magnifying a sign in the native file to 4x, which reads
  "WILMINGTON — Alpine Events — Olympic Winter Games 1980".
- The only large purpose-built resort on Commons, Resorts World Catskills in Monticello, carries its
  wordmark and lion device twice plus a directory sign reading CASINO & HOTEL / VALET PARKING.

## What these frames do not deliver — read before publication

The condo family rule asks for "authentic destination-specific resort or condominium
exterior/grounds". **Neither approved frame contains lodging of any kind.** They deliver:

- destination proof (both are documented, named Catskills locations),
- the region's signature cues (mountain ridgelines in both; peak autumn foliage in the alternate),
- premium, vacation-selling photography,
- perfect compliance (nothing in either frame can imply a property, because there is no property
  in either frame),
- generous overlay real estate.

They do **not** deliver the product cue. Copy and layout must carry the "condo" half of the
proposition, because the photograph will not. Do not caption either image in a way that implies a
view from an accommodation, a resort setting, or an on-site amenity — the frames are a public
reservoir shoreline and a public mountain escarpment.

If the certificate requires a building in the hero, **CON-CAT is unresolved at zero budget** and
should be escalated, not solved by nominating one of the rejected frames below.

## Publication notes

1. **C1 — strip all source metadata.** Re-caption both files generically, e.g.
   `catskills-hero-1600x900.jpg` / alt "Autumn in New York's Catskill Mountains". Do not carry
   "mountain range during golden hour", "a scenic view of a mountain in the fall", the Unsplash
   filenames, or the photographer strings into the site. No property is named in either image, but
   the rule is absolute.
2. **Naming the location is safe; naming a property is not.** The Ashokan Reservoir and the
   Catskill Mountains are public geography, not lodging brands. Captioning the region is fine.
3. **No attribution obligation** under the Unsplash License, and no share-alike. Both approved
   files are therefore cleaner than every Commons candidate, all of which were CC BY-SA.
4. **No retouching was performed and none is required.** Neither file contains a mark to remove.
5. **Upscaling:** none. Primary 0.2665x, alternate 0.4444x — both comfortable downscales.
6. **Seasonality:** the primary is late-spring/early-summer golden hour; the alternate is peak
   October foliage. If the certificate runs an autumn promotion, swap them.
