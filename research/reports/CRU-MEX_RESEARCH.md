## Price conclusion

Statistics computed on the **12 in-window, non-holiday verified rows** (row 13 excluded).

- **Low verified comparable:** **$662** (2 adults, all-in) — Carnival *Radiance*, 4 nights, Long Beach → Catalina → Ensenada, 01/31/2027 (row 7)
- **Median verified comparable:** **$1,072** (mean of the 6th and 7th ordered values, $1,006 and $1,138)
- **Defensible premium comparable:** **~$1,450** (upper quartile = $1,456; three independent 4–5 night Cozumel oceanview rows cluster at $1,432 / $1,432 / $1,464)
- **Highest verified comparable:** **$1,896** — RCI *Liberty of the Seas*, 4 nights, Galveston → Cozumel, 11/05/2026 (row 8)
- **Recommended comparison amount:** **$1,450**
- **Why it is defensible (cite rows):** $1,450 is the upper-quartile of twelve verified, currently-published, taxes-included oceanview fares for two adults and sits inside a tight three-row cluster — rows 1 ($1,464), 2 ($1,432) and 3 ($1,432) — spanning two cruise lines, two ships and both 4- and 5-night lengths, so it is not a single-sailing artifact and is well below the $1,896 high (row 8). It is a genuine premium rather than an outlier: it is 35% above the median (row set median $1,072) but 24% below the highest verified fare, and each of the three anchoring rows is a public retail fare from a provider open to any consumer.
- **HVM charges:** **$314** | **+ deposit: $464**
- **Savings (charges only):** **$1,136** ($1,450 − $314) → consumer-facing rounding: **~$1,135**
- **Savings (charges + deposit):** **$986** ($1,450 − $464) → consumer-facing rounding: **~$985**
- **Confidence: High** on the range and on the recommended amount (12 verified rows, 2 independent providers, 2 cruise lines, 5 ships, both Pacific-Baja and Gulf-Yucatán itineraries, one exact-sailing cross-check within $1). **Medium** on any single sailing's price, which cruise lines reprice daily.
- **Refresh by:** **December 8, 2026** (90 days), and mandatorily re-verified before any new campaign flight, since none of these fares is held.

## Risk notes

1. **Port cannot be promised.** The certificate says "Do not promise Cozumel, Ensenada, Cabo San Lucas, or Costa Maya," yet every verified comparable is priced on a specific named itinerary. The comparison is defensible as *region-level* pricing only; no creative may name the ports the comparable rows used.
2. **Line, ship and length are not guaranteed.** The recommended $1,450 is anchored on 4- and 5-night sailings. A traveler placed on a 3-night sailing has a materially cheaper real-world equivalent — the verified 3-night rows are $954 and $884 for two. If the certificate is routinely fulfilled at 3 nights, $1,450 overstates the like-for-like comparison and the figure should drop toward ~$950.
3. **Two providers only.** Nine cruise-line and agency sites would not serve prices to an automated fetch (403/503/502/JS-only). The set is real and cross-validated but narrower in provider diversity than ideal.
4. **Costco Travel rows (8–12) come from a membership retailer.** The prices are the cruise lines' published fares (proven by the $731 vs $732 match against iCruise on the identical sailing), not member-only discounts, but if a strict reading of "exclude loyalty-only rates" is applied, drop rows 8–12. The recommended $1,450 **survives that deletion**: it is anchored on iCruise rows 1, 2 and 3, all open to the public.
5. **Prices are unheld snapshots.** Cruise fares reprice daily and every row is a single point-in-time observation. Any published comparison must carry the research date, which Display A does.
6. **The existing on-page range "$750–$1,900" is now wrong at the low end.** Verified floor is $662. Either widen the range to "$660–$1,900" or replace it with the single $1,450 reference figure.
7. **Row 13 is a holiday sailing** (Thanksgiving week) and was excluded. If the certificate's listed holidays also include Presidents' Day, row 2 (02/15/2027) must be dropped as well; recomputing without it moves the upper quartile only from $1,456 to ~$1,449, so the $1,450 recommendation is unaffected.
8. **Image licensing carries an attribution obligation.** CC BY 2.0 requires visible credit to the photographer. The site's existing `certificates/artwork-credits.html` link satisfies this only if the specific creator, title, source URL and licence are listed there.

- **None for the price research.** The $1,450 recommendation is fully sourced.
- **One open dependency, inherited, not introduced here:** deposit treatment (baseline Finding 3) is unresolved, so only the conservative $985 savings figure may be published today.
