## What I actually saw at 100 % (native 3447 px and the shipped 1600 px file)

Cropped every low structure out of the native file and magnified 2x–6x, then re-checked the same regions on the shipped 1600x900 at 2x. **No legible text anywhere in either crop.** Item by item: the swim-up bar has a dark flat roof, green columns and a light fascia — the only mark is a ~10 px (native) blue/white circular pictogram on the centre column, no letters, ~6 px delivered, unresolvable. The white pedestal at the pool edge is a plain statue plinth, no lettering. Loungers are plain white plastic with blue-and-white striped cushions, no brand. Palapas are plain thatch. The cream wing at left (and the matching wing at right in the twowing crop) carries balustrades, a roof pergola and windows — **no nameplate, no flag, no rooftop sign**. The windsurf sails are plain red/white panels with no readable logo at 1:1. The gazebo pier is unmarked. The white pickup on the beach (twowing crop only) shows no door lettering at 5x; it is a vibe blemish, not a brand, and the primary crop excludes it. People are small and unidentifiable. Saleability: strong — saturated pool blue, clean sky, sea horizon, palms; this is the "sell the vacation" frame the project has been failing to find.

## The caveat (stated once)

This is **one property** (Iberostar Rose Hall Beach, 2011 state), not a many-property strip. The pixels carry no name; the filename and Flickr/Commons titles do — **never carry the title, filename or alt text to the site (rule C1)**. Caption generically ("Rose Hall, Montego Bay"). Precedent for approving an unnamed single-property frame: the Sedona approval (C1). A genuine multi-property elevated strip of Montego Bay does not exist in free stock at usable quality — see the rejects below; the closest (aircraft-window approach shots, cruise-deck Freeport view) fail on saleability and resolution, not on branding.

## Fetch-mechanics corrections for IMAGE_BRIEF_V2

- **Wikimedia thumbnail widths have changed.** 640 / 800 / 1024 now return HTTP 400 "Use thumbnail sizes listed". The served set per mediawiki.org/wiki/Common_thumbnail_sizes is **20 / 40 / 60 / 120 / 250 / 330 / 500 / 960 / 1280 / 1920 / 3840**. 500 px worked for triage; even that tripped 429 after ~35 serial fetches at 0.8 s spacing.
- Commons originals still 429 on roughly every other request even at 4–6 s spacing with the project User-Agent; exponential backoff to 60 s recovered every file except two, which came back on a second pass.
- **Flickr now 429s original (`_o`) fetches** after ~25 CDN pulls; the `_k` 2048 size still served with a browser UA after a 45 s pause.
- Unsplash Location field again decided geography before download: three "Jamaica resort" aerials read Ocho Rios / Runaway Bay / Ocho Rios and were skipped unopened (C6b).
