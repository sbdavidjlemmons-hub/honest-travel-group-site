## Risk notes
- **The card's current retail band is not supported.** The page displays "$700–$1,900 for 2 adults in an outside cabin." Verified research puts the band at **$578–$1,684**. Both ends are wrong in the direction that flatters the offer. Recommend replacing with **$580–$1,700**, or with the single $900 figure from Display A.
- **The holiday blackout list is not published on the page.** The certificate excludes "listed holiday sailings" but the list is not on this page or linked inline. Any comparable that returns on a holiday is therefore un-priceable against the certificate — this is why row 12 (Mardi Gras, returns Memorial Day 2027) is excluded from the basis despite being an otherwise perfect match. Publish or link the list.
- **Bimodal market.** Mainstream oceanview inventory (Carnival Glory/Freedom, MSC Seashore, Explorer of the Seas) clusters at $578–$946, while new-build mega-ships (Utopia of the Seas, Mardi Gras) cluster at $1,666–$1,684 with nothing in between. Any single "comparable trip cost" is therefore a choice about ship class, not just about date. Because the certificate guarantees **no line, ship or itinerary**, a comparison priced off the mega-ship tier would not be defensible; $900 deliberately sits below it.
- **Single-feed concentration.** Nine of ten basis rows come from CruiseCheap/iCruise, which share an inventory feed. Row 1 was independently reproduced on iCruise and row 12 came from a different retailer (The Cruise Web), but a genuinely independent second feed for the full set has not been obtained. This is the main reason confidence is Medium rather than High.
- **Tax-inclusion ambiguity.** Sailing pages say "Includes taxes & fees"; site fine print says the opposite. Totals here take the inclusive (lower, conservative) reading. Never present these figures as "fare plus taxes."
- **Volatility.** Cruise fares for sailings 1–8 months out move materially week to week and by cabin inventory. The Utopia rows already show interior pricing *above* oceanview, which is an inventory artifact, not a stable relationship. A figure this old will not survive a compliance challenge; hold to the 90-day refresh.
- **Excluded from research on purpose:** Disney Cruise Line (Port Canaveral 4-night oceanview lead-ins run several times the mainstream band). Including Disney would inflate the comparison against a product tier the certificate cannot deliver. Also excluded: flash sales, loyalty/casino offers, group and interline rates, and any interior or balcony pricing.
- **Image share-alike.** The recommended primary is CC BY-SA 4.0. If the site's policy is to avoid share-alike obligations on marketing assets, use the CC BY 2.0 alternate instead, or the CC0 fallback.
