# AIR-SJU / san-juan-airfare-credit / "San Juan Airfare Credit" — image acquisition, V3

**Second attempt, run under the corrected rule in `research/IMAGE_BRIEF_V2.md`: _shoot the
destination, not the aeroplane._** V2 concluded that destination-specific airfare imagery for
San Juan was impossible under a free licence and settled for a geographically neutral
Mediterranean silhouette. **That conclusion was wrong, and this sweep overturns it.**

Assignment: image only. **No pricing work performed. No certificate page, production image, or
any other repo file was touched.** The only file written is this report.

Method actually used, in the order the brief prescribes: search **Puerto Rico**, then inspect the
sky. Queries were destination-led throughout — *San Juan Puerto Rico*, *Old San Juan*, *Puerto
Rico sunset*, *Isla Verde Puerto Rico*, *Condado beach San Juan*, *Puerto Rico beach*, *San Juan
Puerto Rico sky*, *puerto-rico*, *puerto-rico-sunset*, *isla-verde-puerto-rico*,
*old-san-juan-la-perla*. Aircraft-word queries were used only as a **supplementary** discovery
pass late in the sweep (`puerto rico airplane`, `san juan puerto rico plane`) and every frame they
returned was still judged on the frame, not the query. Both nominations below came out of the
destination-led pool.

**43 new candidates were downloaded and opened with the Read tool.** None of the 19 candidates
adjudicated in `AIR-SJU_IMAGE_V2.md` was re-opened. Evidence files (downloads, magnified crops,

## The San Juan finding, corrected

V2's recorded finding was: *"there is no usable free-licence aircraft photograph taken at or over
San Juan."* **That is now shown to be false.** Two exist, both on Unsplash, both free-tier, and
both were found by searching for the place and then looking at the sky:

- `photo-1613845968442` — an airliner over Castillo San Cristóbal, not mentioned in the
  photographer's own alt text or tags, which is precisely why keyword search never surfaces it.
  It is only discoverable by opening destination photographs and examining the sky.
- `photo-1693878981296` — a light aircraft over a Puerto Rican flag, tagged "airplane" and
  therefore reachable by an aircraft-word query, but composed as a picture of the flag.

What V2 got right and this sweep confirms: **Caribbean aviation photography on the stock platforms
is concentrated at Maho Beach, Sint Maarten, and every Maho frame carries a fully legible airline
wordmark.** The way past that is not to search harder for aircraft; it is to stop searching for
aircraft.

What this sweep also confirms: **the base rate is brutal.** Twenty-seven individually opened,
genuinely-San-Juan photographs had a completely empty sky. Two frames out of forty-three carried a
usable aircraft. The method works, but only if the reviewer is willing to open and look at dozens
of frames that will turn out to contain nothing.

## Recommendation, and the honest trade-off

**Use `photo-1613845968442` as the primary, at the crop box given above.** It is the only frame in
the free-licence pool that is simultaneously (a) unmistakably San Juan, (b) carrying a real
airliner, and (c) carrying no legible marking of any kind. It clears the brand bar without being
retouched, and it does not need a geotag bought at the cost of image quality.

**Use `photo-1693878981296` as the alternate.** It is a stronger, larger, more certainly anonymous
aircraft in a much cleaner overlay field, but a weaker aircraft *type*, a flatter sky and a worn
flag.

**If the brand standard is tightened** so that a dark-red tail counts as "distinctive livery" even
with no logo, then the primary fails, the alternate is promoted, and the second slot falls back to
V2's generic sunset silhouette (Pexels 1076033) — which remains a perfectly good, brand-safe image
that simply does no destination work. **That fallback is still available and nothing in this
report requires it to be discarded.**

## Fetch mechanics — confirming the brief's notes

- `commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php` returned **HTTP 429 with `x-envoy-ratelimited: true`** on the
  first request of the session, with a correct User-Agent. The brief's warning is current.
  `WebFetch` against Commons search pages worked and was not rate-limited, exactly as documented.
- Pexels and Unsplash **search pages** rendered fine through `WebFetch` and returned complete result
  lists with photo IDs / `photo-<hash>` filenames; plain `curl` against
  `images.pexels.com/photos/<ID>/...` and `images.unsplash.com/photo-<hash>` returned 200 every time.
  The two-step method in the brief is correct and was used throughout.
- Useful addition to the brief: **`images.unsplash.com/photo-<hash>` with no query string returns the
  full native original** (3820 × 5730 here). That is how the 22× brand check on the primary was
  possible; a `?w=2400` fetch would have destroyed the aircraft before it could be examined.
- Also worth recording: **Unsplash photo pages expose stated location and camera make/model**, which
  Pexels does not. For any brief that needs a verified geotag, Unsplash is the stronger source.
