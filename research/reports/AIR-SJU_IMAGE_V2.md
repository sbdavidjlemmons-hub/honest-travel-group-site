## Search coverage, and the San Juan-specific finding

Searched Pexels and Unsplash hardest, per the standing lesson that they beat Wikimedia on this
brief. Queries run: *airplane landing tropical*, *airplane caribbean*, *airplane sunset ocean*,
*airplane palm trees*, *airplane tropical sunset*, *san juan puerto rico airplane*.

**Finding worth recording: there is no usable free-licence aircraft photograph taken at or over
San Juan.** The Pexels query "san juan puerto rico airplane" returns 23 results and **not one
contains an aircraft exterior** — they are El Morro, the Capitol, Old San Juan streets,
coastline aerials, and one cabin-window shot of the San Juan coast (Pexels 5868550, which fails
the airfare clause outright as a window shot). The Caribbean aviation photography that does
exist on these platforms is concentrated almost entirely at **Maho Beach, Sint Maarten**, and
Maho photography is a genre built specifically to show the airline: every frame I opened from
there (5769706, 34700478, Unsplash 1551963837) carried a fully legible wordmark or registration.
The one Caribbean non-Maho frame I found (Unsplash 1639369492909, St Thomas) failed on shore-side
signage and power lines rather than on the aircraft.

So the honest position: **a literal SJU / Puerto Rico aircraft image is not available under a
free licence at this standard.** The primary and alternate solve the assignment the way the
brief's own logic says to solve it — silhouette and underside framing, real aircraft, zero
brand risk, generous overlay space, and in the alternate's case palm trees carrying the
tropical cue — rather than by lowering the brand bar to buy a Puerto Rico geotag.

## Licence and provenance notes

All four approved candidates are **Pexels License**: free for commercial use, no attribution
required, no share-alike, no attaching obligations to a crop. Nothing CC BY-SA was approved.

**EXIF caveat, stated rather than glossed.** Pexels strips camera metadata from every file it
serves. I fetched the un-resized originals of all five finalists and every one returns no
`Make`, no `Model`, and no exposure block; two retain only an `Artist` string. The brief's
AI-screen therefore cannot be run as written on any Pexels file. What can be checked is the
second half of the test — the *round pixel dimension* — and none of the approvals trips it:

- **1076033 — 4256 × 2392.** 4256 is the native sensor width of the Nikon D3/D700/D300 generation
  (4256 × 2832); 2392 is exactly that frame cropped to 16:9. Coherent camera provenance.
- **2990971 — 4032 × 3024.** Exactly a 12 MP handset's native 4:3 frame. Coherent phone provenance.
- **36226264 — 8436 × 5624** and **21299280 — 2607 × 4000.** Neither is a generator's output
  geometry; 2607 is a side-crop of a 4000-tall frame.

None are the 1024/1536/2048-square or clean-16:9 geometries that generated images arrive in. I
am satisfied on provenance, but flagging the stripped-EXIF limitation so the next reviewer knows
it was a reasoned call and not an unexamined pass.
